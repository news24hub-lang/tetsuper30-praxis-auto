NEP 2020 me 5+3+3+4 structure kya hai? - Sahi Jawab

Correct Answer: NEP 2020 ka naya school structure

National Education Policy 2020 ne 10+2 system ko khatam karke 5+3+3+4 ka naya structure diya hai. 5 saal ka Foundational Stage (3 saal Anganwadi + 2 saal Class 1-2), 3 saal ka Preparatory Stage (Class 3-5), 3 saal ka Middle Stage (Class 6-8) aur 4 saal ka Secondary Stage (Class 9-12). Iska focus rote learning se hatkar conceptual aur skill-based learning par hai.
