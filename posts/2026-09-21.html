Q1. What is 25% of 400?

A) 25 B) 50 C) 100 D) 200

Correct Answer: C

Detailed Explanation: 25% means 1/4th. 400/4 = 100. This type of question is frequently asked in TET and SBI exams. Remember the trick and concept.

Q2. Average of 1 to 10?

A) 5 B) 5.5 C) 6 D) 4.5

Correct Answer: B

Detailed Explanation: Sum 1 to 10 = 55, Average = 55/10 = 5.5 This type of question is frequently asked in TET and SBI exams. Remember the trick and concept.

Q3. A train 150m long crosses a pole in 10 sec. Speed?

A) 15 m/s B) 54 km/h C) Both D) None

Correct Answer: C

Detailed Explanation: Speed = Distance/Time = 150/10 = 15 m/s. 15*18/5 = 54 km/h. So both A and B correct. This type of question is frequently asked in TET and SBI exams. Remember the trick and concept.

Q4. Simple Interest: P=1000, R=10%, T=2 years?

A) 100 B) 200 C) 300 D) 150

Correct Answer: B

Detailed Explanation: SI = P*R*T/100 = 1000*10*2/100 = 200 This type of question is frequently asked in TET and SBI exams. Remember the trick and concept.

Q5. If SP= 500, Profit=20%, CP=?

A) 400 B) 416.66 C) 450 D) 500

Correct Answer: B

Detailed Explanation: CP = SP*100/(100+Profit%) = 500*100/120 = 416.66 This type of question is frequently asked in TET and SBI exams. Remember the trick and concept.
